# What's new in v1.2.1, for someone upgrading from v1.2.0

This release has two parts. On Linux, the tray icon now shows your real usage the first
time it is drawn, instead of the grey no-data ring. On Windows, the panel and the
right-click menu move differently: they slide out of the taskbar as one piece, and the
breakdown, the threshold list and the stat tiles animate their changes instead of jumping.
Nothing changes at rest, so the panel looks exactly as it did in v1.2.0 once it has settled.

## Linux: the tray icon waits for the first reading

On Linux the tray icon is now put on the desktop only once the first reading has been drawn
into it, so a notification area that looks at the icon straight away sees the real gauge
rather than the grey ring. It waits at most 5 seconds. If no reading is ready by then, the
icon is shown anyway, and with no data the ring is still grey, as before.

This is aimed at a grey icon reported after launch on a GNOME desktop, where the icon could
stay grey for up to a minute until the next poll. See the last section for how far that fix
has been checked.

## Windows: the panel and the menu slide in and out as one piece

The panel and the right-click menu now slide out of the edge they are docked to and slide
back, taking 460 ms to open and 300 ms to close, with the border and the rounded corners
leading. The old wipe, the small shift of the content and the fade are gone, so the window
stays fully opaque throughout.

- **A click mid-slide turns it round.** Clicking while the panel is opening makes it close
  from where it had got to, and opening it while it is closing does the same in reverse.
  It does not jump to an end and start again.
- **Text stays sharp.** Each frame is placed on a whole screen pixel, so text in Display
  mode does not shimmer on the slow end of the slide.
- **A click on the tray icon while the panel is closing leaves it closed.** The click is
  read as the second half of the toggle, as before.

## Windows: the Bars / Breakdown switch slides and the table fades in behind the fold

- **The highlight slides** between Bars and Breakdown instead of jumping, and the two
  labels crossfade.
- **The fold takes 400 ms to open and 280 ms to close.** The table's rows fade in one after
  another behind the fold, each rising a few pixels, and on the way out they are gone
  before the fold closes.
- **A second click at any point ends in the state it asked for.** Three faults that were
  already in v1.2.0 are fixed. A click in the middle of a fold made the panel jump to full
  height. A fold replaced by a second click could finish with the table open but its rows
  invisible and the switch on Bars. A click that reversed a closing fold made it stand
  still and then jump.
- **The fold no longer overshoots.** It used to open about 6 pixels too far and drop
  back in one frame; it now stops at the right height.

## Windows: the right-click menu's threshold list folds and its ticks draw on

- **The threshold list folds open and closed** instead of jumping, in 340 ms and 240 ms,
  with its rows fading in one after another. A menu dismissed during that 300 ms wait does not
  fold the list afterwards.
- **A tick draws itself on and fades off.** A tick caught half way carries on from what is
  on screen rather than starting again.
- **Choosing a threshold shows it first.** The pill updates at once, the chosen dot grows in,
  and the list folds away about 300 ms later. Choosing a second time restarts that wait.
  Clicking the pill during the wait closes the list straight away.
- **Highlights fade.** The hover and focus fill on menu rows, option rows and the pill fades
  in and out. A press is still instant.

## Windows: a stat tile's flip is a crossfade

Clicking a stat tile used to swap its total for its breakdown in one frame. Now the total
lifts out, the breakdown rises in behind it, the bar draws from left to right, the legend
entries follow one by one, and the arrow turns half a circle. The tile never changes size
during the flip, and the hover and focus fill fades the same way as in the menu. A second
click turns the tile round where it is.

## If you turn Windows' animations off

All of the motion above follows the Windows setting for animation effects, read each time a
transition starts. With it off, the panel and the menu appear and disappear at once, folds
and flips happen in one step, and nothing is ever caught half way. Linux has no motion and
is unchanged.

## Smaller things

- **The speed is one setting in the code.** Every duration and curve for the panel and the
  menu now lives in one file with the pace as a single value, at 2 for this release. It is
  not something you can change.
- **A new command-line check, `--motion-check`,** drives a tile and the threshold list on
  real windows and reports whether each ends where it should, including after a second
  click. `--fold-check` gained the case of a click that reverses a close and now ends with
  PASS or FAIL. `--menu-check`'s line about the open list now tests something that can
  fail. `--motion-check` works beside a running O-view; `--toggle-check` and
  `--popup-check` still do not.
- **A test that failed at random no longer does.** `tools/check.ps1` used to fail now and
  then on the scope-note test, because another test class changed a shared setting while it
  ran. That class now runs alone. Nothing a user sees changes.
- **The documentation describes the new motion.** The UI spec, the overview, the
  architecture notes, the command-line reference and the feature checklist were updated,
  with two new checklist rows: animations off, and a second click ending in the state it
  asked for.

## What's known not to work, or hasn't been checked

- **The Linux tray fix has not been seen on a real desktop.** It is shown against a
  stand-in tray host that reads the icon the moment it registers, and by CI's headless
  Linux job with a real session bus. Nobody has watched it on the GNOME desktop where the
  grey icon was reported, so whether it is gone there is not known.
- **The cause of the grey icon is the best route found, not an observed one.** GLib drops a
  signal that arrives before a tray host has finished its own set-up, and the tray extension
  Ubuntu 24.04 ships (58-1ubuntu24.04.1) does not read the icon again after that. The fix
  keeps O-view from offering grey in that window. If the first reading takes longer than
  5 seconds, the grey ring is shown and the reading follows as before.
- **Maximilian signed off the Windows motion on a test build on 2026-11-06, with no
  notes, but no agent tried the following on it.** A real mouse pointer over the tiles, rows
  and menu (the agents used keyboard focus and raised clicks instead). The real Windows
  animation setting switched off (the agents forced it off in a temporary build). Any
  display scale other than 100%, so whole-pixel movement at a fractional scale has not been
  seen. A taskbar docked at the top. A panel dragged away from the taskbar, which should
  slide out of its own window edge, wherever that is on screen. Whether he tried these is
  not recorded.
- **`--toggle-check` and `--popup-check` have not been run on this release.** They exit
  without a word while an installed O-view is open, and none was closed for them. The
  tray-click-while-closing behaviour above is therefore read from the code and traced, not
  covered by that check.
- **The Windows head has no automated test of its motion.** What repeats is the three
  command-line checks, which a person has to run on a desktop, and CI does not run them.
  The per-frame figures behind the changes came from temporary probes that were removed
  before each merge.
- **Two small jumps were measured on the test build and left alone.** For one sampled frame
  at the end of a closing fold the panel's bottom edge lifts by 11 to 16 px. A menu
  dismissed while its threshold list is folding open grows 9 px past its docked edge. A
  smaller step of about 6 pixels also remains at each end of the breakdown's fold.
- **A quick double click on a tile can feel hesitant.** A second click 100 ms into a flip
  holds the returning side for the 100 ms start delay before it rises. If a click arrives
  while the bar is still drawing, the bar and legend carry on under the side that is
  leaving.
- **Smoothness on a very slow machine is unknown.** The panel is resized and moved on every
  frame for twice as long as before. The traces show even steps, but they were taken on one PC.
- **The screenshots in the docs are from 2026-10-23,** before the model-limit bar and the
  new motion. They are due a refresh in the next maintenance slice.
- **Not re-checked in this release:** the very first Alt+arrow press right after clicking
  the panel's header was reported as doing nothing in v1.2.0, and no change here was aimed
  at it.
