# What's new in v1.3.0, for someone upgrading from v1.2.1

This release redraws the Windows panel. It is now two flat blocks, "Your plan" and "On
this machine", with a 31-day chart, one table you switch between Output and Overall, a
second face that shows every model on two doughnuts, a view the panel remembers, and
motion on all of it. **The Linux panel is not part of this redesign.** It keeps the layout
it had in v1.2.1, and nothing in this release gives it the new one. The tray icon, the
right-click menu and the numbers behind them are unchanged.

## Windows: "Your plan" sits flat on the panel

The session, weekly and per-model meters now run the full width of the panel with no box
around them, under a "YOUR PLAN" caption and above one soft line.

- **Off-plan spend is the block's last row.** It was a separate section at the foot of the
  panel. It now sits under the meters with the `credit-billed` chip, a caption (`est. credit
  spend · last 31 days`, or `reported by Claude` when the figure is Claude's own), the
  amount, and its coverage caveat underneath.
- **The off-plan notice can be hidden.** A small `×` on its top right, labelled "Hide this
  notice", closes it, and the panel closes up over the gap. It stays hidden when you close
  the panel and when you restart O-view. It comes back only for a new off-plan spell, after
  a poll has read the account as no longer off-plan in between.

## Windows: "On this machine" is a chart, a table and a footer

The four stat tiles, the "Tokens used" section with its Bars / Breakdown switch, and the
boxed graph are gone from the Windows panel. In their place is one flat block.

- **A 31-day chart with a date axis.** One bar per day, fading from faint on the left to
  full at today, with today's bar in the accent colour and `today` written under it. A line
  stands on every Monday with its date. Days before you installed draw no bar. The dotted
  reset lines and their hover cards are gone from the Windows chart.
- **One table.** Centred letter-spaced headings, soft row and column lines, and a bold
  total. In Overall, the estimated value sits in one pill across each pair of columns.
- **A footer** with the single caveat that applies on the left and the Output | Overall
  switch on the right.
- **Compact density** scales the chart and the gaps to fit. The sample state for a short
  display now renders Overall, because the Output table is short enough that the panel no
  longer needed to scroll there.

## Windows: Output | Overall

The switch in the footer changes the chart and the table at once.

- **Output** counts output tokens and prices only those.
- **Overall** counts everything billed (input, cache writes, cache reads and output) and
  prices everything, with a row for each kind of token.
- While you are off-plan, the value row reads `Est. spend` instead of `Est. value`. A value for
  a model with no known price is shown as unknown, never as zero.

## Windows: the model face

A small `by model` tag at the top right of "On this machine" turns the block over to two
doughnuts and a table. The tag then reads `totals`, and pressing it turns the block back.

- **Two doughnuts**, one for today and one for the last 31 days, each with its total and
  unit in the centre. Pointing at a section opens a card with the model's name, its figure,
  its share (`output · 37% of 31 days`) and its estimated value.
- **Every model in the store has a row.** Nothing is folded into "other". A model with no
  known price reads `unpriced`, and a model that did nothing today but is priced reads a
  known `$0.00`. A long model name is trimmed in the table, and the full id is in the row's
  tooltip.
- **Colours.** The three largest models get the three colours, and every later model gets a
  grey that steps towards the background. A model has the same colour on both doughnuts and
  in the table.
- **The Output | Overall switch works on this face too.**

## Windows: the panel remembers its view

Output or Overall, totals or models, and whether the off-plan notice is hidden are saved
with your other settings. The panel opens on the view you left it in, including after a
restart. A fresh install opens on Output, totals, notice shown. Before this release a flipped
tile went back to the summary every time the panel opened.

## Windows: the new motion

- **Output and Overall.** The lit segment of the switch slides between the two, the chart's
  bars move to the other series, and the table folds, with the old rows fading out and the
  new rows fading in and rising a few pixels.
- **On opening,** the plan bars grow to their level and the chart's bars rise from the
  baseline.
- **The block turns over.** "by model" and "totals" fade one face into the other while the
  height moves between them. Each doughnut fills in one clockwise sweep with its sections
  appearing as it passes, and the table rows fade in behind it.
- **Pointing at the block** brightens the tag, gives its arrows a half turn and draws a
  faint outline round what would turn. Hover cards fade in.
- **A second click turns things round from where they are.** A press during a fold or a turn
  ends in the state that press asked for, and the panel stays on its docked corner through it
  all.
- **If you turn Windows' animations off,** every one of these happens at once, read each
  time a transition starts. The panel opens settled, with the bars and doughnuts already at
  their full size.

## Smaller things

- **New command-line checks.** `--view-check` presses the switch and the tag on a real panel,
  closes it mid-fold, and reads the view back from the settings file. `--still-check` forces
  animations off and checks that nothing is left running. `--fold-check` and `--motion-check`
  now cover the new fold and turn, and `--popup-samples` and `--tile-samples` were rewritten
  for the new layout. See `docs/cli.md`.
- **A check now fails the build if anything in the Windows panel is clipped.** It looks at
  every sample state in both themes, the compact and short renders, and the hover cards. The
  one exemption is a model name in the table's name column, and only while its full id is in
  a tooltip. It runs inside `tools/check.ps1` and in CI's render job.
- **New sample states** the redesign is drawn against: Overall, by model, off-plan and
  hidden, the widest figures, a model face with a 47-character model id, and the switch caught
  part-way through a press.
- **The old tile code is deleted.** Nothing in the new layout used it.
- **Linux sample pictures drew the "Tokens used" bars as slivers.** In the off-screen renders
  and the pictures in `docs/images`, the four segments came out about 1 px wide each. A live
  Linux panel was already drawing them at full width, so nothing changes for anyone running
  the app. The 26 Linux renders and the four Linux pictures in the docs now show what the
  panel draws.
- **The documentation describes the new Windows panel.** The UI spec, the architecture notes,
  the command-line reference, the overview and the README were updated, the Windows panel
  pictures were retaken from the current renders, and the feature checklist gained six rows
  (F57 to F62) for the plan block, the totals face, the model face, the remembered view,
  hiding the notice and nothing clipped. F09, F12, F14 and F16 are now marked as Linux's,
  with every proof kept.

## What's known not to work, or hasn't been checked

- **Linux has none of the redesign, and nothing was checked on Linux for it.** The Linux panel
  keeps its v1.2.1 layout, with the four tiles, the "Tokens used" section and the dotted-line
  graph. A Linux version of the new design is a later release.
- **Maximilian signed off a test build of this code on 2026-11-08, on his own Windows
  machine, at one display scale.** It was built from the same code as this release, which has only these notes and the README
  changed since. Nothing
  has been tried at another display scale or above 60 Hz, by him or by an agent.
- **The agents drove the panel with raised events, not a real mouse.** Nobody has recorded
  watching the hover fades on the switch and the tag, the hover cue as a pointer crosses the
  block's edge, or the doughnut card's fade. Whether he tried them is not recorded.
- **The agents' checks of the remembered view did not restart O-view.** `--view-check` and the
  engine's tests rebuild a panel from the settings file, and no second process was started.
  A real quit and restart was one of the checks on the sign-off card Maximilian accepted on
  the test build on 2026-11-08, and the card is all that records it.
- **The hide control on the off-plan notice was not pressed on the assembled app.** Only a
  throwaway probe with a stand-in for the settings saved pressed it, and `--view-check`
  carries the hidden flag through a file without pressing the `×`. The sign-off card asked
  for it only if the notice was showing on his account, and nothing records that it was.
- **A press while the old rows are still leaving was tried once.** A temporary timing change
  drove it and it ended in the right state, but no check shipped in the app lands there: every
  second click in `--fold-check` comes after the old rows have gone.
- **Switching Output and Overall can recolour a model.** The three colours go to the largest
  models on the measure you have chosen, so a model's colour can change between the two. This
  is a design call that was left open and accepted.
- **A Monday in about the last five days of the chart has its line but no date.** The date
  would otherwise run into `today` or past the panel's edge. This is accepted too.
- **A 31-day Overall total can read in millions, not billions.** The formatter stops at M, so a
  very large total reads `4733.4M tokens` rather than `4.7B`.
- **A long model id in today's hover card is calculated to stay inside the panel, not seen.**
  With a 54-character id its right edge works out at about 2530 on a panel running to 2548
  device pixels. The 31-day card was shown. No golden shows a trimmed name; one probe did.
- **A press that moves the panel into compact density was not run.** The chart changes height
  there, so the bars snap to their end instead of moving.
- **`--toggle-check` and `--popup-check` have not been run on this release.** `--toggle-check`
  exited 0 but wrote no report, so it is not counted as run. They exit silently while an
  installed O-view is open. `--menu-check` last passed at the change that removed the old
  tiles and was not re-run on the final commit.
- **One frame at the end of the Overall table folding shut is not understood.** `--fold-check`
  records a frame with the panel's bottom edge 5 to 10 px above its docked edge, corrected
  within 7 ms. Nobody has established whether that frame reaches the screen.
- **How it feels at full speed on a slow machine is unknown.** The traces show even steps,
  taken on one PC.
- **The live Linux bars may end 1 px past the end of their meters.** The four rounded segments
  add up to 367 px on a 366 px track, as measured once on Windows with Avalonia's own window,
  not in an X11 window. Nobody has measured these bars on a real Linux desktop.
- **The desktop checks are not in CI.** Only `--popup-samples` runs in `tools/check.ps1` and CI.
  `--fold-check`, `--motion-check`, `--view-check` and `--still-check` need a person at a
  desktop session.
- **Not re-checked in this release:** the first Alt+arrow press after clicking the panel's
  header, reported as doing nothing in v1.2.0. No change here was aimed at it.
