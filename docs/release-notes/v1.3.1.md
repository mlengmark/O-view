# What's new in v1.3.1, for someone upgrading from v1.3.0

This release gives the Linux panel the redesign v1.3.0 gave Windows. On Linux the panel is
now two flat blocks, "Your plan" and "On this machine", with a 31-day chart, one table you
switch between Output and Overall, a second face that shows every model on two doughnuts,
a hideable off-plan notice, and a view the panel remembers. **Nothing about the Windows
panel changed.** No Windows source file and no Windows sample picture differs from v1.3.0.
The Linux tray icon, the right-click menu and the numbers behind them are unchanged too.
**The Linux panel has no motion:** every change below happens at once, with no slide, fold
or fade.

## Linux: "Your plan" sits flat on the panel

The session, weekly and per-model meters now run the full width of the panel with no box
around them, under a "YOUR PLAN" caption and above one soft line.

- **Off-plan spend is the block's last row.** It was a separate section at the foot of the
  panel. It now sits under the meters with the `credit-billed` chip, its caption and the
  amount. The longer explanation that used to stand under it is now the row's hover text.
- **The off-plan notice can be hidden.** A small `×`, labelled "Hide this notice", closes the
  notice at once and the panel closes up over the gap and stays where it was. It stays hidden
  when you close the panel and when you restart O-view, and comes back only for a new
  off-plan spell, by the same rule Windows uses.
- **The meters' fill is now a share of the track as drawn.** It used to overrun the track by
  2 px at 100%, and it stays true in the narrower column a scroll bar leaves.
- **A boost chip wraps instead of running under its figure.** It has its own column, as on
  Windows. On Windows it trims, on Linux it wraps, because the widest promotion does not fit
  one line in Ubuntu's wider font.

## Linux: "On this machine" is a chart, a table and a footer

The four stat tiles, the "Tokens used" bars with their Bars / Breakdown switch, and the
"Usage · last 31 days" graph are gone from the Linux panel. In their place is one flat block.

- **A 31-day chart with a date axis.** One bar per day across the panel's width, fading from
  faint on the left to full at today, with today's bar in the accent colour and `today`
  written under it. A line stands on every Monday with its date. Days before you installed
  draw no bar. The 31 rotated dates, the dotted weekly-reset lines and their hover cards,
  and the shading by week are gone. Each bar keeps its hover text.
- **One table** under the chart, for today and the last 31 days, with the single caveat that
  applies on the left of the footer. The coverage line (`10 of 31 days recorded`, and a note
  for a model with no known price) stands once under the table.
- **Every word and figure is the Windows panel's.** Linux reads the same view models, so the
  two panels cannot disagree about a number.
- **A short display compacts the panel.** The chart and the gaps scale down, and a panel
  taller than the screen still scrolls.

## Linux: Output | Overall

The switch sits in the footer. A press changes the figures, the chart and the table at once.

- **Output** counts output tokens and prices only those.
- **Overall** counts everything billed (input, cache writes, cache reads and output), with a
  row for each kind of token, its share of the total, and an estimated value in one pill
  across each pair of columns.
- While you are off-plan, the value row reads `Est. spend` instead of `Est. value`. A value
  for a model with no known price is shown as unknown, never as zero.
- **The panel stays where it was** when you press, and on a screen short enough to make the
  panel scroll, a press keeps the foot in view instead of jumping to the top. The keyboard's
  place returns to the segment you chose.

## Linux: the model face

A small `by model` tag at the top right of "On this machine" turns the block over to two
doughnuts and a table. The tag then reads `totals`, and pressing it turns the block back.

- **Two doughnuts**, one for today and one for the last 31 days, each with its total and
  unit in the centre. Pointing at a section opens a card with the model's name, its figure,
  its share and its estimated value. The 31-day doughnut's card opens to its left, so it
  stays inside the panel.
- **Every model in the store has a row.** Nothing is folded into "other". A model with no
  known price reads `unpriced`. A long model name is trimmed in the table and the full id is
  in the row's tooltip.
- **Colours.** A model has the same colour on both doughnuts, in the table and on its card.
  The fourth model and later take a grey.
- **The Output | Overall switch works on this face too**, and a press on the tag on a
  scrolling panel keeps the tag where it stood in the view.

## Linux: the panel remembers its view

Output or Overall, totals or models, and whether the off-plan notice is hidden are saved
with your other settings. The panel opens on the view you left it in, including after you
exit O-view from its menu and start it again. A fresh install opens on Output, totals,
notice shown.

## Smaller things

- **A check now fails the build if anything in the Linux panel is clipped.** It looks at
  every sample state in both themes, the short and compact renders, and fails on anything
  drawn past the panel's edge, any line of text that trims, and any table cell that wraps.
  The one exemption is a model name in the model table, and only while its full id is in a
  tooltip. It runs in CI's Ubuntu leg, so it uses Ubuntu's wider font.
- **The Linux short render is now the model face**, `panel-offplan-on-models-short`, the
  tallest state, so the picture that proves the panel scrolls on a short screen still
  scrolls. A new `panel-typical-compact` render shows the compact density.
- **The old Linux tile code and its tests are deleted.** Nothing in the new layout used them.
- **The documentation describes the new Linux panel.** The architecture page, the UI spec,
  the overview and the README were updated, the four Linux pictures in `docs/images` were
  retaken from the current renders, and the feature checklist now lists Linux for F57 to
  F62. F09, F12, F14 and F16 say no panel draws them any more.

## What's known not to work, or hasn't been checked

- **Maximilian signed off a test build of this code on an Ubuntu VM on 2026-11-09, with one
  Yes to a card of checks.** The test build was `1.3.1-trial.1`, built from the same code as
  this release, which has only these notes and the README changed since. The card listed
  these checks, and the Yes covers the card as a whole; nothing records which items he tried
  or what each one showed. A first Yes at 09:28 that morning came with a screenshot of the old
  panel, because the VM was still running the old build, so it counts for nothing here.
- **The off-plan notice's `×` has no record of being pressed on a real Linux desktop.** The
  card asked for it only if his account showed the notice, and nothing records that it did.
  Before the VM, a test and a throwaway probe on Avalonia's Windows backend pressed it.
- **The `×` has no keyboard route on Linux.** On Windows it is a button. On Linux the
  Output | Overall segments take focus, but there is no visible focus ring on them or on the
  `by model` tag.
- **Hover text on a chart bar and on a model's name is plain black on white**, in both
  themes. The Linux panel registers no theme, so these tooltips are not styled. Only the
  doughnut's card draws its own surface. No sample picture shows a hover card on Linux.
- **There is no motion on Linux.** Windows' animations are not part of this release, and no
  later one is promised here.
- **Anything that needs a window was measured on Avalonia's Windows backend, not on X11.**
  That covers hit tests on the doughnut's sections, where the cards open, and the panel's
  height changing when you press the switch or the tag. The 31-day doughnut's cards are
  pinned to open to the left, and a card from the left doughnut opens at the pointer, which
  was proved by measurement and not watched.
- **A switch press, a tag press or a hide on a screen short enough to make the panel scroll
  is covered by tests and renders, not by a window.** A test that reads the window's source
  holds the window's half. The VM's screen height is not recorded.
- **On Ubuntu's fonts, the off-plan panel in Overall fits a 712 px work area**, so a screen
  that size may show no scroll bar there, which would be correct. Only the sample picture
  is pinned to the scrolling layout, and the panel's height on Ubuntu was not measured.
- **A Monday in about the last five days of the chart has its line but no date**, so the date
  does not run into `today` or past the panel's edge.
- **The documentation still says the panel has not been seen on a Linux desktop.** That was
  true when it was written. The README and `docs/platform-status.md` have not been updated to
  record the VM check.
- **Not re-checked in this release:** Windows. Nothing in it was aimed at the Windows panel,
  and its sample pictures are unchanged, but no one ran the Windows panel again for v1.3.1.
